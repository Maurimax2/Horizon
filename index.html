Why Mauritania

There are places you visit.
And places you remember forever.

Mauritania is the size of Egypt with fewer visitors than a single European city receives in a weekend. Almost nothing here has been arranged for tourists — which is precisely the point.

You come for the emptiness, the caravan towns that have not moved in eight centuries, the tea that takes three hours, and a night on a freight train that no brochure can prepare you for.

Chinguetti & Ouadane

A city built by caravans.

Chinguetti's libraries hold manuscripts that have been copied, corrected and guarded by the same families for centuries. They are not museums. Someone unlocks the door, and hands you the book.

Journeys that include the ancient cities

Richat Structure

The Eye of the Sahara.

Forty kilometres wide, made of concentric rings of different rock, formed by geological and magmatic processes over millions of years. From the ground it is a landscape. From orbit it is an eye.
