Why Mauritania

There are places you visit.
And places you remember forever.

Mauritania is the size of Egypt and receives fewer visitors in a year than a single European city takes in a weekend. Almost nothing here has been arranged for tourists — which is precisely the point.

13:00 — Nothing in any direction

Two hundred kilometres to the next anything.

No fence, no pylon, no engine but yours. The horizon does not get closer. This is the part people try to describe afterwards and can't.
