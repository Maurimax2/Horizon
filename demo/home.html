Richat Structure

The Eye of the Sahara.

Forty kilometres across, with rings so distinct they are used as a landmark from orbit. Long believed to be an impact crater, it is now read as a deeply eroded geological dome. We reach it by 4×4 from Ouadane and camp on the ridges, facing the centre.
